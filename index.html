Delivery you can follow

From the seller to your door in four steps, with a handover code so nothing is marked delivered until you say so.

  1. 1

    Request

    Choose delivery on your order. You see the fee before you pay.

  2. 2

    Rider matched

    The nearest approved rider is offered the job. Declined? The next one gets it.

  3. 3

    Pickup and transit

    The rider collects from the seller and heads to you. Follow the status.

  4. 4

    Confirm with your code

    Check the item, then give the rider your 4-digit code to complete delivery.

Where we deliver Delivery opens area by area, once each has approved riders.
How is my rider chosen?

SafeBuy NG offers each job to one approved rider at a time, starting with the nearest. If a rider declines or does not answer, the next rider gets it. Only one rider can ever hold a job.

What does delivery cost, and who gets paid?

The fee depends on whether pickup and drop-off are in the same city, the same state or different states, and you see it before you pay. You pay the fee to SafeBuy NG, and the rider is paid only after delivery is confirmed.

What if no rider is found?

You will see “No rider yet” while our team steps in. If we cannot find one, you get a full refund of the delivery fee, or a free retry if you prefer.

Who can ride with SafeBuy NG?

Individual riders and delivery companies can apply. We check each rider’s ID and documents before anyone can take a job.
